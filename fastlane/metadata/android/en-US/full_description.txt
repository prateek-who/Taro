<p><b>Taro</b> tracks your steps, food, weight and sleep in one place and works out how much you really burn. It learns that from your own data, so your daily calorie target gets more accurate the longer you use it. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads and no internet permission.</p><ul><li>Steps, distance, streaks and goals with hourly, daily and yearly charts</li><li>Food logging with about 7,700 built in foods, your own meals and recipes, and a nutrition label scanner that works offline</li><li>Energy burned through the day, a calorie target for cutting, maintaining or bulking, and a model that learns your real daily burn from your food logs and weight</li><li>Weight trend with water swings filtered out, weekly rate and a forecast</li><li>Sleep estimated from phone use, with averages and bedtime consistency</li><li>Around 160 badges, including calisthenics skill and strength trees</li><li>Full backups to a folder you choose, widgets and a quick settings tile</li></ul>
